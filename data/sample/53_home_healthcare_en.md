# Home Healthcare & Nursing (EN)

Following discharge from an inpatient admission, home nursing care is covered for up to 30 days per event with a physician's order. Home nursing is reimbursed up to SAR 400 per day at network providers.

A home doctor visit is covered up to 4 visits per event for members who are bedbound or recovering from major surgery. Medical equipment rental (oxygen concentrator, hospital bed) is covered when prescribed, up to SAR 3,000 per event.

Home healthcare requires prior authorization and a care plan reviewed every two weeks. Chronic long-term custodial care that is non-medical (help with daily living only) is not covered.
