# Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation (EN)

Physiotherapy is covered up to 15 sessions per policy year with a referral from the treating physician. Each session is reimbursed up to SAR 250 at network centers.

Sessions beyond the 15-session limit require prior authorization and a documented rehabilitation plan showing measurable clinical progress. Post-surgical rehabilitation and stroke rehabilitation may qualify for an extended course.

Home-based physiotherapy is available for members who are medically unable to travel after major surgery, capped at 10 home visits per event. Occupational therapy follows the same session limits as physiotherapy.
