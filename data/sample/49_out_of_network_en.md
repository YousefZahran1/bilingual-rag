# Out-of-Network Reimbursement (EN)

Care received from a provider outside the network is reimbursed at 60% of the approved CCHI tariff for the service, not 60% of the amount actually billed. The member is responsible for any difference above the approved tariff.

Claims for out-of-network care must be submitted within 60 days of the service date, with the original itemized invoice and a copy of the medical report. Claims submitted after 60 days may be rejected.

A genuine emergency treated at the nearest out-of-network facility is processed as in-network, with no reduced-reimbursement penalty, until the member is stable enough to transfer to a network hospital.
