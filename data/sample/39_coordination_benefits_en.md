# Coordination of Benefits (Dual Coverage) (EN)

When a member is covered by two plans (for example, their own employer plan and a spouse's plan), one plan pays first as the primary payer and the other pays second as the secondary payer.

For an employee, their own employer plan is primary. For a covered spouse, the spouse's own employer plan is primary and this plan is secondary. For a child covered under both parents, the plan of the parent whose birthday falls earlier in the calendar year is primary.

Secondary claims must be submitted within 90 days of the primary plan's settlement, together with the primary plan's explanation of benefits. Total reimbursement from both plans combined never exceeds 100% of the actual eligible expense.
