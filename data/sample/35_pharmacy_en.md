# Outpatient Pharmacy & Medication Coverage (EN)

Prescription medications obtained from network pharmacies are covered at 80% of the approved price; the member pays the remaining 20% as coinsurance. The annual pharmacy benefit is capped at SAR 10,000 per member.

Generic substitution is mandatory where a therapeutic equivalent exists. A brand-name drug is only reimbursed at the generic rate unless the treating physician documents medical necessity for the brand.

Chronic-disease medications (for diabetes, hypertension, asthma, and similar conditions) may be dispensed in 90-day supplies with a single copay, provided an active chronic-care registration is on file.

Specialty and high-cost drugs (biologics, oncology support, injectable therapies above SAR 2,000 per unit) require prior authorization before dispensing. Over-the-counter products without a prescription are not reimbursed.
