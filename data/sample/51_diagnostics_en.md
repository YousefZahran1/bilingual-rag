# Diagnostics, Laboratory & Radiology (EN)

Routine laboratory tests ordered with a physician referral are covered at 100% in-network. Basic radiology (X-ray, ultrasound) is covered at 100% in-network with a referral.

Advanced imaging (MRI, CT, PET) requires prior authorization and is subject to an annual advanced-imaging cap of SAR 20,000 per member. Requests are reviewed within 48 hours; urgent inpatient requests are reviewed within 4 hours.

Genetic and molecular testing is covered only when it changes clinical management and is pre-approved. Repeat imaging of the same site within 90 days requires justification to avoid duplicate-service denial.
