# Oncology & Cancer Treatment (EN)

Chemotherapy and radiotherapy delivered at network facilities are covered at 100% after prior authorization, subject to an annual oncology cap of SAR 500,000 per member.

A second medical opinion for a new cancer diagnosis is covered before treatment begins. Targeted and immunotherapy agents require formulary approval and documented evidence that standard therapy is unsuitable or has failed.

Supportive care (anti-nausea medication, growth-factor injections) associated with an approved treatment plan is included within the oncology benefit and does not consume the separate outpatient pharmacy cap. Palliative care is covered where curative treatment is no longer indicated.
