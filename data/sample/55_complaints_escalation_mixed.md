# Complaints & Escalation / الشكاوى والتصعيد (mixed AR/EN)

If a claim is denied or a service is delayed, the member should first contact the insurer's customer care / خدمة العملاء within the app or call center. The insurer must acknowledge a written complaint within 5 working days / خمسة أيام عمل and issue a final response within 20 working days.

If the member is not satisfied, the complaint can be escalated to the Council of Health Insurance (CCHI) / مجلس الضمان الصحي التعاوني through its official channels. The member should keep the complaint reference number / الرقم المرجعي للشكوى and all supporting documents.

للحالات الطارئة التي تُرفض خطأً، يمكن طلب مراجعة عاجلة (urgent review) — the insurer must respond within 24 hours / خلال 24 ساعة. Retaliation against a member for filing a complaint is prohibited.
