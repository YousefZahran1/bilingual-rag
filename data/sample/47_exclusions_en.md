# General Exclusions (EN)

The following are not covered under the standard plan: cosmetic and aesthetic procedures unless required to restore function after an accident; cosmetic dental work; refractive eye surgery for lifestyle reasons.

Also excluded: injuries resulting from self-harm; treatment for substance abuse outside an approved program; experimental or unproven treatments not registered with the Saudi FDA; and non-prescription supplements and vitamins.

Injuries sustained during professional (paid) sports, and any claim arising from war, invasion, or nuclear contamination, are excluded. Services obtained without required prior authorization may be reduced or denied. An exclusion does not remove the member's right to emergency stabilization.
