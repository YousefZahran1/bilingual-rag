# Preventive & Wellness Benefits (EN)

One comprehensive annual health check-up is covered at 100% with no copay, including basic blood panel, blood pressure, and BMI assessment. Members are eligible for one check-up per policy year.

Vaccinations follow the Saudi Ministry of Health schedule and are fully covered in-network, including the seasonal influenza shot once per year. Childhood immunizations for covered dependents are covered at 100%.

Cancer screening is included: women aged 40 and above are covered for a mammogram once every two years, and colorectal screening is covered for members aged 45 and above. No prior authorization is required for standard preventive screenings performed at network providers.
