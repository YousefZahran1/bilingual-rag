# Maternity & Newborn Benefits (EN)

Antenatal visits and routine pregnancy screening are covered at 100% in-network with no copay. Normal (vaginal) delivery is covered up to SAR 12,000; medically indicated cesarean section is covered up to SAR 18,000.

There is no separate waiting period for maternity when the member transferred from a continuous prior employer plan; otherwise a 30-day enrollment condition applies before delivery benefits begin.

The newborn is automatically covered for the first 30 days under the mother's policy. To continue coverage, the newborn must be enrolled as a dependent within 30 days of birth.

In-vitro fertilization (IVF) is not covered under the standard plan. The premium tier covers up to two IVF cycles per lifetime, subject to prior authorization.
