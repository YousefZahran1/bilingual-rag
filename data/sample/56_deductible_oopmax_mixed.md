# Deductibles & Out-of-Pocket Maximum / التحمل والحد الأقصى للنفقات (mixed AR/EN)

The plan has an annual deductible / التحمل السنوي of SAR 500 per member that applies before coinsurance begins, except for preventive care and emergencies, which are exempt / معفاة.

After the deductible, the member pays coinsurance / نسبة التحمل المشترك (typically 20% in-network) until reaching the annual out-of-pocket maximum / الحد الأقصى للنفقات السنوية of SAR 15,000 per member (SAR 40,000 per family). Beyond that maximum, the plan pays 100% of eligible in-network costs for the rest of the policy year / بقية سنة الوثيقة.

Amounts paid for out-of-network care above the approved tariff, and any non-covered service, do not count toward the out-of-pocket maximum / لا تُحتسب ضمن الحد الأقصى للنفقات.
