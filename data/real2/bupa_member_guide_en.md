## Page 14

How to know my benefits?
Refer to the Table of Benefits enclosed with your pack to know all the details of benefits you
are entitled to. We advise you to register in our online services by visiting member.bupa.com.sa
to get all the details of your benefits.
What is the duration of my policy?
The policy duration should be from the activation of the Policyholder contract, not from the
inception date of the member.
Which hospital or clinic can I go to for treatment?
You can go to any hospital or clinic included in the list enclosed in this pack. There is a chance
that your list has been updated from the first time you received it. To get an updated one, visit
our website at member.bupa.com.sa.
Can I add a specific hospital/clinic.
We can't add a medical provider after activating the contract. According to the agreement with
the policyholder, the provider network is entitled to this.

Can I go to a hospital not included in my
network?
Yes, if you are eligible for reimbursement, you can go to any
hospital, clinic or doctor of your choice outside your hospital
network. However, you will have to pay for all costs directly to
the hospital or clinic. You can then file for reimbursement later,
using the Bupa claim form, which you can download at
member.bupa.com.sa. This will allow you to claim from us the
amount you are eligible for according to your network price.
Note: The reimbursement will be based on customary NW prices.

## Page 15

I’m going on vacation outside the Kingdom; can I use my
Bupa Arabia card?
Yes, you can use your card at any of the specific providers that have prior contracts with with
us when traveling according to your network. Also, depending on your coverage if you are
eligible for reimbursement outside KSA, you can get treatment at any hospital while on
vacation or on business at any provider of your choice. However, you will have to pay for all
costs directly to the hospital. You can then file for reimbursement and will be compensated as
per our policy.
What is claiming (reimbursement)?
This is the process of submitting the cost of medical services provided outside of your
approved network of hospitals and clinics and settling it with us based on the terms and
conditions of your insurance policy. You need to file your claim form with Bupa within 90 days
of your treatment.
What does ‘reasonable and customary’ mean?
Reasonable and Customary means that all the claimed medical fees and expenses are
compatible with our negotiated fee-for-service with providers in your assigned network of
hospitals and clinics in Saudi Arabia. Hence, we will match the billed cost of any medical service
done outside your network with the negotiated fee-for-service cost of the same service if it was
done in your network based on the services rate agreement with our providers.
What are the treatments, medications or procedures not
covered?
Based on CHI policy and on international practices and standards, there are certain treatments,
medications and procedures that are not covered.
Additional information
No SMS is delivered for requests with “Cancelled”, “Acknowledged” and “Approvals” after
referral.
Why I am being paid less than what I have spent?
Bupa Arabia pays amounts based on Bupa Arabia's contractual and discounted price of the
same service inside your same medical network provider.
We highly recommend to use our medical network to avoid incurring any additional costs form
paying directly to the providers.
Why do I have Rejections?
There are multiple reasons for rejections, most common reason is the presenting of an
uncovered service according to your table of benefits, also presenting a service that may not be
approved by FDA or the SFDA, Medications that are not listed in the SFDA.
If there is service done without justification, prescription or without a medical report to explain
the need for, it may also be rejected.


## Page 16

Why is my claim pending?
The claim is pending because there is an important document missing to proceed, you would
receive an SMS that describes the missing documents.
Common causes include absence of the Tooth number in dental claims which is needed to
record on our system. Absence of the eye test for Optical claims.
Another very common cause is the absence of the "Proof of payment" when a credit invoice is
submitted with no indications of payment is available, also the unavalability of medical
prescription or medcial report to clarify the diagnosis or the condition.
Why is my submitted invoice rejected?
An official invoice must be a formal Itemized Electronic invoice or if manual it should have an
invoice number, VAT registration number a with signature and stamp. It is also very important
that
all services paid for are itemized and clear in order for us to identify the rendered services and
pay for them accordingly.
What is the 90 Days Submission protocol policy?
According to the contractual agreement Between Bupa Arabia and your company, any claim
should be submitted within a specified period from the treatment date. Submitting a claim after
that specified period would result in rejection of the claim. The period designated for claim
submission is usually 90 days for the majority of companies, however, this may vary between
one company and another.
How can I update my IBAN?
You can update your IBAN through Bupa Arabia mobile application at any time.
